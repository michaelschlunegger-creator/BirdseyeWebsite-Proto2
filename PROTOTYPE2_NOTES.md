# Birdseye Prototype 2

## Purpose

Show Dextra how the existing flying-drone opening can lead into Birdseye's complete service portfolio. Preserve the strongest original motion, the orange and black identity, the supplied logos and the detailed service visuals.

## Changes in Prototype 2

| Location | Change | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage opening | Existing Birdseye drone video with broad asset-value headline | Keep the eye-catching opening while explaining the wider business |
| Opening solution links | Four equally prominent, illustrated links to Indoor, Outdoor, Digital Engineering and Assessment | Visitors can enter the right service without waiting for the film |
| Homepage structure | Move the four solution choices into the opening and remove the repeated lower card block | Keep the journey direct |
| IDEA wording | Explain digital records and engineering models in plain language | Help non-specialist buyers understand the output |
| Video controls | Pause/play, reduced-motion preference and still-image fallback | Keep motion optional and text readable |
| Media | Copy the original video and a still frame into this repository | Avoid dependence on the original website at runtime |
| GitHub deployment | Separate workflow and `/BirdseyeWebsite-Proto2` asset path | Keep Prototype 1 and Prototype 2 independently shareable |

## Guidance for Dextra

Preserve the existing site's strongest motion effects and combine them with the new content, structure and visuals. Refine transitions, responsive layout and image crops so the website feels coherent. The four solution areas must have equal prominence; indoor drone footage must not imply that Birdseye only offers indoor inspections.

All detailed service pages, industry pages, case studies, portraits and biographies are carried forward from Prototype 1. The separate review log's proposed factual and functional corrections are still open; this homepage revision does not close those items. In particular, the inherited enquiry form remains a prototype and does not deliver enquiries.
